## Fact sources

- Apollo coordinates, dates, sites and sample masses (their sum is 381.72 kg): NSSDCA [landing site coordinates](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/lunar/lunar_sites.html), [Apollo lunar landings](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/lunar/apolloland.html)
- Apollo 12 and Surveyor 3: [NASA](https://www.nasa.gov/history/55-years-ago-apollo-12-makes-a-pinpoint-landing-on-the-moon/)
- Tycho and Copernicus coordinates and diameters: [Gazetteer of Planetary Nomenclature](https://planetarynames.wr.usgs.gov/), 85.29 km and 96.07 km. Other sources give about 93 km for Copernicus
- Tycho's age, about 109 million years and contested: [Lund University](https://www.science.lu.se/node/685)
- Mare Orientale basin, about 930 km: [Zuber et al., via PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7462089)
- Chang'e 4, 177.6E 45.5S, January 2019: [The Planetary Society](https://www.planetary.org/articles/change4-success)
- Luna 3: [NASA Science](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/first-photo-of-the-lunar-far-side/)
- The far side gets as much sunlight, new moon lights it fully: [StarChild](https://starchild.gsfc.nasa.gov/docs/StarChild/solar_system_level2/dark_side.html), [NASA Moon facts](https://science.nasa.gov/moon/facts/)
- Far side lacks maria and is crowded with craters: [NASA Science](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/the-moon-from-the-other-side/)
